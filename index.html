How it works

  • Title Case capitalizes major words and keeps small words (a, an, the, and, of, in, to…) lowercase unless they start or end the title or follow a colon.
  • Sentence case capitalizes the first letter of each sentence and the pronoun "I".
  • Words with internal capitals, like iPhone or NASA, are left alone when your text is mixed case.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never uploaded.
